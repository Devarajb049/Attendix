About Attendix

MITS Attendance Tracer — Free student attendance analytics

Attendix (also known as MITS Attendance Tracer) is a free web application built specifically for students of Malla Reddy Institute of Technology and Science (MITS) to quickly and accurately check their attendance from the official IMS portal, analyze subject-wise data, and plan their schedules effectively.

Why Attendix?

The official MITS IMS portal can be slow, session-heavy, and difficult to navigate on mobile. Attendix solves that by providing:

  • ⚡ Instant scraping — attendance fetched directly from IMS in seconds.
  • 📊 Visual analytics — bar charts and doughnut risk distributions.
  • 🎯 Bunk Advisor — calculates exactly how many classes you can skip or must attend to reach your target (75%, 80%, 85%, or 90%).
  • 📄 PDF Reports — download a clean, printable attendance report.
  • 📱 PWA & Mobile App — install on Android or iOS home screen for instant access.
  • 🌙 Dark / Light Theme — comfortable for any environment.
  • 🔒 Privacy First — credentials go directly to the MITS IMS server and are never stored externally.

How It Works

  1. You enter your MITS register number and IMS password.
  2. Attendix uses a headless Playwright browser to authenticate with the official MITS IMS portal on your behalf.
  3. Your attendance data is scraped, parsed, and returned securely to your browser session.
  4. Your credentials are never logged, stored in a database, or sent to any third party other than the MITS IMS server.

Technology Stack

  • Backend: Python · FastAPI · Playwright (headless Chromium)
  • Frontend: Vanilla HTML / CSS / JavaScript · Chart.js · jsPDF
  • Deployment: Vercel (web) · Docker
  • Mobile Companion: Android (Jetpack / Glance widgets) · iOS (WidgetKit)
