How to Install on Your Device

Android
iOS / iPhone
Desktop
  1. 1 Open Attendix in Chrome or Edge browser on Android.
  2. 2 Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top right corner.
  3. 3 Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. 4 Confirm by tapping Install to launch as an app!
  1. 1 Open Attendix in Safari on iPhone or iPad.
  2. 2 Tap the Share button () at the bottom.
  3. 3 Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. 4 Tap Add in the top right to place on your home screen.
  1. 1 Open Attendix in Chrome, Edge, or Brave browser.
  2. 2 Click the Install icon in the browser address bar.
  3. 3 Or click browser menu (⋮) → Save and Share → Install Attendix.
  4. 4 Launch Attendix anytime from your desktop or taskbar!
