Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026  |  Attendix — MITS Attendance Tracer

This Privacy Policy describes how Attendix ("we", "our", or "the application") collects, uses, and handles information when you use our web application at attendix-dev.vercel.app.

1. Information We Collect

  • MITS IMS Credentials: Your student register number and IMS password are submitted by you to authenticate and scrape your attendance from the official MITS IMS portal. These credentials are transmitted directly to the MITS IMS server and are not stored on Attendix servers or any third-party database.
  • Local Storage: With your consent ("Remember credentials"), your credentials may be stored in your browser's localStorage solely on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you submit it to fetch attendance.
  • Attendance Data: Attendance records scraped from the MITS IMS portal are displayed in your session. We do not permanently store your attendance data on our servers.
  • Usage Data: Standard server logs (IP address, browser type, page accessed, timestamp) may be retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes. This is standard practice for web services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you with the MITS IMS portal and retrieve your attendance.
  • To display analytics, bunk advice, and PDF reports within your session.
  • To maintain basic server logs for security monitoring and error diagnosis.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Attendix uses browser localStorage to optionally remember your login credentials and theme preference on your device. No third-party tracking cookies are set by Attendix itself. If Google AdSense is enabled, Google may set cookies as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

Attendix may display advertisements served by Google AdSense on public-facing pages. Google AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to show relevant ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ad Settings.

Advertisements are placed only on public informational pages. No ads are placed on login screens or alongside sensitive attendance data.

Ad approval status: Ad serving is pending Google AdSense approval. Ads are not currently live.

5. Data Security

All communication between your browser and Attendix uses HTTPS. Credentials are sent directly to the official MITS IMS server and are not logged or stored by Attendix. We implement standard security headers (Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, X-XSS-Protection) to protect users.

6. Third-Party Services

  • MITS IMS Portal: Your credentials are submitted to the official MITS portal. Their privacy practices apply.
  • Google Fonts & FontAwesome: Used for typography and icons. These CDNs may log access metadata.
  • Chart.js, jsPDF: Loaded from CDN for charts and PDF generation.
  • Google AdSense: May be used for advertising on public pages. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel: Hosting platform. See Vercel Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

Attendix is designed for college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not store personal data beyond server logs, there is no persistent data to delete. If you use browser localStorage to remember credentials, you can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use our Contact page.
